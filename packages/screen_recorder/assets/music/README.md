Each release page lists CC0. License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Legal text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en
CC0 permits copying, modification and redistribution, including commercial use.
Artist credits are retained voluntarily; no endorsement is implied.

`sources.json` records the direct downloads, retrieval date, original and bundled
SHA-256 checksums, durations, and format conversion. Audio is full-length AAC
stereo (48 kHz, 256 kbps), with no added or generated music. Presets start at 20%
volume and do not repeat by default; the user may enable looping explicitly.

Imported copies are content-addressed in the app's support directory. Old
projects referencing one of the seven exact withdrawn generated preset hashes
have that track cleared when opened; custom imports are preserved.
