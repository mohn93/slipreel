Slipreel vs Loom

The Loom alternative for demos worth keeping.

Loom is built for firing off quick async videos. Slipreel is built for the polished, edited demo — automatic zoom, smoothed cursor, device frames, and captions — exported as an MP4 or GIF that lives on your Mac.

Download for macOS See how they compare

Loom and Slipreel solve different halves of the same job. Loom is a cross-platform, cloud-first way to record a quick video and drop a share link into Slack — great for async updates. Slipreel is a native macOS tool for producing a genuinely polished demo: it auto-zooms on your clicks, smooths the cursor, frames the app on a background, and burns in captions, then exports a finished MP4 or GIF that never leaves your Mac unless you choose to upload it.

If your goal is a produced-looking demo you own, here is how the two stack up — and when Loom is still the right call.

Slipreel vs Loom: common questions

Is Slipreel a good Loom alternative?

Yes, if you want polished, edited demos you own. Slipreel adds automatic zoom, a smoothed cursor, device frames, and local MP4 or GIF export. Loom is the better pick when you want instant cloud sharing, cross-platform recording, and a team video library for quick async messages.

Does Slipreel work like Loom's instant share links?

Not exactly. Slipreel exports a finished MP4 or GIF file to your Mac rather than hosting videos behind a link. You then share that file however you like. Loom is built around instantly shareable hosted links.

Is Slipreel cheaper than Loom?

Slipreel is $12/month, $89/year, or $129 once. Loom is free for up to 25 short videos, then $18 per user per month for Business. For one person producing finished demos, Slipreel's one-time license is cheaper over time.

Does Slipreel upload my recordings to the cloud like Loom?

No. Recording, editing, and captions all run on your Mac and nothing is uploaded. You share the exported file yourself, so you decide where it goes.

Does Slipreel have automatic zoom that Loom doesn't?

Yes. Slipreel detects your clicks and pushes in on them automatically, and smooths the cursor path. Loom does not do cinematic auto-zoom.

Does Slipreel run on Windows like Loom?

No. Slipreel is macOS 13 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel. Loom is cross-platform across Mac, Windows, and mobile.
