Questions

What is Slipreel?

Slipreel is a macOS screen recorder and editor that turns a raw screen capture into a polished product demo. The moment you stop recording it adds automatic zoom-ins on your clicks, smooths the cursor path, wraps the footage in a device frame on a background, and can transcribe captions on-device — then exports to MP4 or GIF. It records the full display, a single window, or a region, along with the microphone, system audio, and your webcam.

Who is Slipreel for?

Anyone who makes product demos, tutorials, or feature walkthroughs on a Mac — founders, product and marketing teams, developer advocates, educators, and support teams — who want a finished, edited-looking video without opening a full video editor.

How do I unlock export after buying?

Verify your email before checkout. After payment is confirmed, a purchase started in Slipreel returns to the app to activate that Mac. If you bought on the website, install Slipreel and choose Sign in in the app. Three full-quality exports are included free; a paid license unlocks unlimited exports on two Macs.

What happens after the first year on a one-time license?

You can keep exporting with versions released during your included update year. Later updates are an optional paid renewal. Online activation renews at least every 14 days, including for one-time licenses.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 13 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

Do my recordings get uploaded anywhere?

Your recording media stays on your Mac for recording, editing, captions, and export. The app connects for license activation and renewal, update checks, and the first caption-model download. Optional analytics, diagnostics, and submitted feedback can also send data; see Settings and our privacy policy. Read our privacy policy.

How do captions work offline?

The Whisper transcription engine ships inside the app and runs on your Mac. Generate captions when you are ready; the speech model is downloaded once on first use (about 488 MB) and cached; after that, captions work with no network at all, and your recording is never uploaded.

Is it safe to install?

Slipreel is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Updates are cryptographically verified before they install.

Can I edit the automatic zooms?

Yes. Every auto-detected zoom is a normal timeline region you can move, resize, retime, or delete, and you can add your own.

Does it record system audio?

Yes, as its own track, separate from the microphone, so you can mix them independently.
