Changelog

Released versions and download availability are listed in Downloads.

1.0.13

9 September 2026

Existing customers need to sign in again after this security update. Both license types require an online license check at least every 14 days.

  • Safer exports protect source recordings and preserve existing destination files if export fails or is canceled.
  • Crash recovery preserves microphone and system-audio tracks and checks recovered media before removing the original.
  • GIF limits and export estimates now follow the edited timeline, including trims and speed changes. Clipboard export copies a file that supported apps can paste.
  • License checks refresh while Slipreel is open and when you return to the app. Update checks explain one-time license compatibility and link to eligible downloads.
  • Checkout verifies your email, preserves your Mac activation details when you cancel and retry, and waits for payment confirmation before activating. Sign-in and checkout pages no longer load analytics.

1.0.12

7 September 2026

This release includes the screen recording and editing workflow, MP4 and GIF export, and on-device caption generation.

Detailed notes for earlier releases were not published. We will include user-facing changes with future releases.
