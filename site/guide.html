1

Install and grant permissions

Download Slipreel for macOS, open the disk image, and drag Slipreel into Applications. Open it from Applications. Slipreel requires macOS 13 or later on an Intel or Apple Silicon Mac.

Allow Screen Recording when prompted. Enable microphone or camera access if you plan to use them. If macOS asks you to restart Slipreel after changing a permission, quit and reopen it.

2

Record

Choose a display, window, or region. Select your microphone, system audio, and camera as needed, then start recording. Make a short test recording first to check that your intended audio sources are included.

3

Edit

Stop recording to open the editor. Trim the timeline, adjust zoom and cursor styling, pick a background or device frame, and preview the result.

Add captions

Open the captions controls and start generation. The first use downloads a roughly 488 MB speech model; subsequent transcription runs on your Mac without uploading your audio.

4

Export and share

Choose Export, select MP4 or GIF, and save to a new file. GIF exports are limited to 60 seconds of edited content. Open the exported file to check the result, then attach it in your preferred sharing app.

You can record and edit without a time limit and try three full-quality exports free. For unlimited exports, choose a paid plan. Verify your email before checkout. In Slipreel, choose Sign in and open the emailed link to activate this Mac. A license supports two Macs; manage them in your account.

Licensing and updates

The supported download archive starts at 1.0.13. Installers 1.0.0–1.0.12 are withdrawn. Contact support if your one-time license does not cover a supported release.

Paid licenses, including one-time purchases, need an internet connection at least every 14 days to renew activation. A one-time license includes one year of updates. After that, use an eligible version or choose an optional paid renewal. Sign in on the downloads page to find releases covered by your license.

Billing changes

A full refund removes access from the refunded purchase; partial refunds retain access. An open payment dispute suspends the affected purchase while it is resolved. Past-due subscriptions have seven days of grace; canceled or unpaid subscriptions lose access. Changes reach the app when it checks online, or when its saved activation expires within 14 days.

Need a hand?

Email your app version and what happened. You don’t need to send your recording.
