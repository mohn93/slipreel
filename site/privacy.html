Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026 · Becoming Ventures, LLC

This policy explains what data Slipreel (the website at slipreel.app and the macOS app) collects, why, and who processes it. We keep collection minimal and do not sell your data.

Your recordings stay on your device

Screen recordings, camera footage, audio, and the videos you export are processed entirely on your Mac. They are never uploaded to us. They leave your device only when you choose to export or share a file. Captions are transcribed on-device.

Product analytics

We use PostHog (US) to understand how the website and app are used, so we can improve them. This records events like page views and which features are used — not the contents of your recordings, file names, or screen.

  • Website: anonymous, cookie-free page and interaction analytics, plus session replay (a playback of on-page interactions, with typed input masked). No advertising or cross-site tracking.
  • App: anonymous usage events, identified only by a one-way hash of a device identifier — never the raw identifier. This is opt-out: turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy. It never captures recording content.

Account & email

If you purchase Slipreel, we collect your email address to create your license, sign you in, and send transactional email (receipts, sign-in links) via Resend. After a purchase we associate your email and account id with your PostHog analytics profile so we can understand the customer journey. We do not attach your name (we don't collect it) and the app itself never transmits your email.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them.

Hosting & delivery

The site and API are served through Cloudflare and our own servers. Standard server logs (IP address, request metadata) are processed to operate and secure the service.

Cookies

The website does not use cookies for analytics; preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. Essential functionality (such as completing a purchase) may use cookies strictly necessary for that flow.

Your choices

  • Turn off app analytics in Settings → Privacy.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by emailing us.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@slipreel.app, Becoming Ventures, LLC.

This policy describes our current practices in plain language and may be updated as the product evolves.
