Slipreel vs Screen Studio: common questions

Is Slipreel a good Screen Studio alternative?

Yes. Slipreel covers the same core as Screen Studio on macOS — automatic zoom on your clicks, a smoothed cursor with motion blur, device frames on custom backgrounds, on-device captions, and MP4 or GIF export — and adds a one-time lifetime license and fully local processing. The main thing Screen Studio has that Slipreel does not is built-in shareable links for hosting a recording without exporting a file.

Does Slipreel have automatic zoom like Screen Studio?

Yes. Slipreel detects every click and pushes in on it, anticipating where the cursor is heading. Each zoom is a normal timeline region you can move, resize, retime, or delete, and you can add your own.

Is Slipreel cheaper than Screen Studio?

Slipreel is $12/month, $89/year, or $129 once for a lifetime license. Screen Studio is subscription only, at $20/month or $9/month billed yearly (about $108/year). So Slipreel is lower yearly and is the only one of the two with a one-time purchase option.

Do Slipreel's captions upload my recording?

No. The Whisper transcription engine ships inside the app and runs on your Mac; the speech model downloads once on first use and is cached. Your recording never leaves the machine.

Can I try Slipreel before paying?

Yes. Slipreel is free to download and fully functional to record and edit before you buy. Payment only unlocks export.

Does Slipreel run on Windows?

No. Slipreel is macOS 13 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel — the same Mac-only footprint as Screen Studio.
